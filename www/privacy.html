Hydrippo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Hydrippo is a water tracking app made by SideQuest Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Hydrippo on Android.

The short version

Your drinks, goal, bottles and settings stay on your phone. We don't run servers that store them, we don't have accounts, and we don't show ads or use advertising trackers. Two optional features, both off until you turn them on, share a little with others: the hot-day boost sends a rounded, ZIP-area location to the US weather service, and Health Connect sync writes your drinks to Health Connect on your phone.

Information stored on your device

Hydrippo saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • The drinks you log (time, amount, type of drink)
  • Your daily goal and the details you entered to calculate it (weight, activity level, whether you work somewhere hot)
  • Your bottles, awake hours, reminder settings and app preferences

We can't see this information. It is removed when you use Settings > Your data > Erase all data or uninstall the app. Android may include app data in your personal Google device backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Backups you make

If you use Settings > Your data > Save a backup, Hydrippo creates a file with your drinks and settings and hands it to the app you pick (for example Google Drive, Files or email). That file is yours. We never receive it.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone using Android's notification system. No reminder data is sent to us.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Hydrippo widget, it shows today's total on your home screen, so anyone who can see your home screen can see it. Drinks you log with the widget's button are saved on your phone like any other drink.

Hot-day boost (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Automatic hot-day boost and enter a US ZIP code, Hydrippo looks up the approximate center of your ZIP code's area (using a table built into the app) and sends only that rounded location to the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to get today's forecast high. Hydrippo does not use GPS or your phone's location permission. The ZIP code stays on your phone and is never sent to us. The National Weather Service is a US government agency; see https://www.weather.gov/privacy.

Health Connect (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Health Connect sync, Hydrippo writes the drinks you log (the time and the amount) to Health Connect on your phone, and updates or removes them when you edit or delete a drink in Hydrippo. Hydrippo only asks for permission to write hydration data. It does not read any data from Health Connect. Data in Health Connect stays on your phone and is controlled by you in Health Connect's settings, where you can also remove Hydrippo's access at any time. Turning sync off in Hydrippo stops new writes; drinks already written stay in Health Connect until you delete them there.

Hydrippo's use of Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements: hydration data is only used to provide this feature, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

Sharing your streak

If you tap Share my streak, Hydrippo makes a picture of your streak and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Purchases (Hydrippo Plus)

If you buy Hydrippo Plus, the payment is handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service provider, to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives purchase information from Google Play (such as the product you bought, purchase dates and an anonymous app user ID). It does not receive your hydration data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and Google's at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't use your data for advertising.
  • We don't use health or hydration data to make decisions about employment, insurance or credit.

Children

Hydrippo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Medical disclaimer

Hydrippo gives general hydration guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about your own fluid needs.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
