# Hydrippo

A no-ads water tracker from SideQuest Studio. Slide the water line on your own bottle to log a drink; Drip the hippo soaks deeper as you go. Your day ends while you sleep, not at midnight.

- **Try it:** open `www/index.html` in Chrome (use the device toolbar for phone size).
- **Tests:** `npm test` (logic) and `python3 tests/sim/simulate.py` (usage simulator)
- **Android build, billing, release:** read `GUS_HANDOFF.md`.
- **Store listing, privacy policy, Play Console answers, graphics:** `store-listing/`

Fonts: Sniglet and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, both SIL Open Font License (see `www/fonts/`).
