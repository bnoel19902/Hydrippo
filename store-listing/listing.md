## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Hydrippo is a water tracker built around the bottle you already carry. Pick your tumbler, sport bottle, gallon jug or glass, then slide the water line to where your bottle is now. Hydrippo logs the difference. No typing, no guessing cup sizes.

Hydrippo is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It gives general hydration guidance. Talk to a doctor or other healthcare professional about your own fluid needs, and follow their advice if they have given you a limit.

MEET DRIP
Drip is a hippo who loves a good soak. Every sip you log fills his pond a little more. Hit your goal and he's in up to his nostrils, which is exactly where a hippo wants to be.

YOUR DAY ENDS WHEN YOU SLEEP
Most trackers start a new day at midnight. If you work nights or stay up late, that splits your day in two. Hydrippo asks when you usually sleep and rolls your day over in the middle of it, so a drink at 2 AM still counts for the day you were living.

WHAT'S FREE, FOREVER
• Log by sliding the line on your own bottle, with a refill counter
• Edit any drink, or add one you forgot, even yesterday's
• Quick buttons you set to the drinks and sizes you actually have
• A daily goal based on your weight, activity and heat, plus a one-tap boost for hot or heavy days
• The shift-friendly day
• Reminders inside your awake hours that wait after you drink and stop once you hit your goal
• Help for phones that put reminders to sleep
• Your full history and streaks, never locked
• Backup and restore for a new phone
• No ads. No pop-up upgrade screens. No diet talk. No account.

HYDRIPPO PLUS
• Every bottle you own
• 16 drinks and a daily caffeine tally
• Shift schedules: different awake hours on different days
• Insights: best weekday, usual first and last drink, this week vs last
• 30-day chart and CSV export
• Dress up Drip with a cap, a lily or shades
